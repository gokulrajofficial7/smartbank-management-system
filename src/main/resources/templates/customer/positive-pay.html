Positive Pay System (PPS) for Cheques

Reserve Bank of India Guidelines for Cheque Truncation System (CTS) — Circular DPSS.CO.RPPD.No.309/04.07.005/2020-21

Simulated CTS Module
Why Positive Pay System?

The Positive Pay System (PPS) is an automated fraud prevention measure developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) under RBI directives.

  • Cheques of ₹50,000 and above: Availing of PPS facility is at the discretion of the account holder.
  • Cheques of ₹5,00,000 and above: Mandated by SmartBank policy for dispute resolution protection under CTS.
  • CTS Clearing Verification: When the payee presents the cheque for clearing, the details are cross-referenced with your pre-registered confirmation before debit.
Internship Simulation Disclaimer

SmartBank is an educational prototype and simulated software platform for demonstration.

This module showcases realistic adherence to statutory CTS clearing safeguards and data structures prescribed by the Reserve Bank of India, but is not linked to live physical clearing houses or actual NPCI CTS grid servers.
