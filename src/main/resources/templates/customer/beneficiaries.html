RBI Anti-Fraud Security Rule — 4-Hour Cooling-Off Window

In accordance with Indian banking security mandates, any newly added beneficiary is subject to a 4-hour security cooling-off period. During this window, transfers to the beneficiary may be monitored for unauthorized payee tampering. Once the 4 hours elapse, the beneficiary is marked fully verified.
